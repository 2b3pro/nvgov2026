Immigration Deep Dive: 287(g) Agreements, Community Trust & Police Deputization

Published a major research expansion on Section 287(g) federal immigration agreements and the community trust doctrine across the Immigration Issue Guide, candidate profiles, and primary research dossiers.

  • • 287(g) Agency Roster & Detainer Surge: Documented the four active Nevada 287(g) Warrant Service Officer agreements (Las Vegas Metro, Douglas County, Mineral County, and Nye County) and detailed how Metro's June 2025 re-entry contributed to a 30-fold increase in ICE detainer requests (957 through August 2025 vs. 32 in 2024).
  • • The Community Trust Doctrine: Analyzed the public safety tension between local policing and federal deportation, documenting how fear of immigration enforcement deters crime victims and domestic violence survivors from calling 911 and prevents witnesses from cooperating in felony prosecutions.
  • • Dillon's Rule Litigation: Sourced the ACLU of Nevada's landmark lawsuit (Morais-Hechavarria v. LVMPD) asserting local sheriffs lack statutory authority from the Legislature to execute 287(g) agreements—a ruling that could void all Nevada agreements statewide.
  • • Three-Candidate Comparison: Upgraded the issue guide to feature nominees Joe Lombardo and Aaron Ford alongside independent candidate Danielle Ford, adding an 8-row comparison matrix, voter tradeoffs, and town hall debate questions.
  • • Sources Added: Added source citations IMM3 through IMM8 covering ACLU filings, AG Model Policies, LVMPD directives, and campaign platforms.

Our Commitment to Transparency

We believe voters deserve to know when information changes. This page documents:

  • New content — When we add new issues, candidates, or analysis
  • Updates — When we expand or modify existing content
  • Corrections — When we fix errors (with explanation of what was wrong)
  • Source additions — When new evidence becomes available

If you notice something that should be updated or corrected, email errors@nvgov2026.org with the page URL, the claim in question, and any source we should review. Reports are reviewed as time allows, but you may not receive a reply. You can also open a public issue on GitHub.

Disclaimer: This site was created with AI assistance. While we have made every effort to verify claims against primary sources, errors may exist. All factual claims include citations; hover over superscript numbers to see sources, or visit our sources page for complete documentation including PDF dossiers. We encourage voters to verify information independently.

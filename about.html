About This Project

A nonpartisan voter information resource for the 2026 Nevada gubernatorial election.

Our Mission

Democracy works best when voters understand who they're voting for—including the contradictions. This site exists to provide Nevada voters with sourced, analytical information about the general-election nominees, while preserving archived primary research, without endorsing any candidate.

We believe voters deserve to see:

  • The full picture — not just campaign talking points, but actual records and actions
  • The contradictions — where candidates say one thing and do another
  • The money trail — who funds them and how that might influence their governance
  • The steelman arguments — the strongest case for AND against each candidate, fairly presented

Our Methodology

1. Sourcing Standards

  • ✓ Primary sources preferred: Official records, court documents, financial disclosures, legislative votes
  • ✓ News sources for context: Used to understand events, not as sole evidence for claims
  • ✓ Partisan sources labeled: When using campaign materials or party communications, clearly identified
  • ✓ Links provided: Every factual claim links to its source so readers can verify

2. Fairness Standards

  • ✓ Structural equality: Each candidate gets the same page structure and depth of analysis
  • ✓ Contradictions for all: We identify inconsistencies for all candidates, not selectively
  • ✓ Steelman approach: We present the strongest case FOR each candidate before critiquing
  • ✓ Equal updates: When new information emerges, we update all relevant candidate pages. See our recent updates page.

3. Analysis Standards

  • ✓ Fact vs. analysis labeled: Clear distinction between objective facts and our interpretation
  • ✓ Multiple interpretations: When evidence can be read different ways, we acknowledge that
  • ✓ Open to correction: If we get something wrong, we'll fix it and note the correction on our updates page

What This Site Is NOT

Not an Endorsement

We do not endorse any candidate. We present information; you decide.

Not a Prediction

We're not forecasting who will win. We're helping voters understand the choices.

Not Complete

No single source can capture everything. We link to fuller sources for deeper research.

Not Immune to Bias

All analysis involves judgment. We try to be transparent about our limitations.
